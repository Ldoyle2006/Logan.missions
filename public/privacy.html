YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy

This page explains what information this website handles when you sign up for updates, make a donation, or browse the site. It is intended to describe the site’s current setup in plain language.

Last updated October 9, 2026

Email updates

If you subscribe, the form sends your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your first name to Brevo, the email service used to manage the update list and send occasional updates. You must agree to receive these emails before the signup is submitted.

You can unsubscribe using the link in an update email. You can also email Logan to ask for help or request removal from the list. The signup details are sent to Brevo and are not stored in a website mailing-list database.

Donations

For card or bank payments made through the giving form, the form sends Stripe your email address, selected amount, and giving schedule to prepare the payment. Payment details are collected by Stripe through its secure payment form; this website does not receive your card number or bank-account credentials.

Other giving options may take you to EquipNet, Venmo, or Cash App, or open your email app to request bank-transfer instructions. Those services and your email provider handle information you submit to them under their own privacy practices. Please review their notices before sharing information.

Website and service providers

The website does not currently use its own advertising or analytics tools. The hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP address and request details, in routine server logs to deliver and protect the site. Stripe’s payment form and other third-party services may collect information needed to provide their services.

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to run the feature you choose, such as sending updates or processing a donation. This site does not sell your contact information.

Your choices and questions

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. For questions about this notice, or to ask about information submitted through this site, contact Logan Doyle at Logan.missions@icloud.com. Information held by Stripe, Brevo, or another provider may also be subject to that provider’s privacy policy and processes.
