YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy

This page explains what information this website handles when you sign up for updates, make a donation, or browse the site. It is intended to describe the site’s current setup in plain language.

Last updated October 10, 2026

Email updates

If you subscribe, the form sends your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your first name to Brevo.

Read more

Brevo manages the update list and sends occasional updates. You must agree to receive these emails before the signup is submitted.

You can unsubscribe using the link in an update email.

Read more

You can also email Logan to ask for help or request removal from the list. The signup details are sent to Brevo and are not stored in a website mailing-list database.

Donations

For card or bank payments made through the giving form, the form sends Stripe your email address, selected amount, and giving schedule to prepare the payment.

Read more

Payment details are collected by Stripe through its secure payment form; this website does not receive your card number or bank-account credentials.

Other giving options may take you to EquipNet, Venmo, or Cash App, or open your email app to request bank-transfer instructions.

Read more

Those services and your email provider handle information you submit to them under their own privacy practices. Please review their notices before sharing information.

Website and service providers

If you allow optional analytics, this website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-FVB86SM3MB) and Microsoft Clarity (project ID yvw7ivzevg) to understand site usage.

Read more

Neither tool loads unless you choose “Allow analytics.” Google receives page-view events containing only the page path, page title, and a query-free referrer path; this website does not configure analytics events that send newsletter fields, donor names, email addresses, payment information, donation amounts, or Stripe events. Microsoft Clarity records interactions and session playback to help understand how the site is used. The newsletter signup and donation sections are marked for masking in Clarity, and form inputs are masked by default. Google Analytics property settings can independently enable Enhanced Measurement events, including form interactions and outbound-link clicks; those settings are controlled in Google Analytics, not on this website. To keep measurement limited to page views, form interactions and outbound-link clicks should remain disabled in the property. Google and Microsoft may process technical information such as IP address and browser/device data when their tools run. See Google’s privacy policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement for details.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser and applies to both tools across the site.

Read more

You can reject analytics or change your choice below. The hosting provider may also process basic technical information, such as IP address and request details, in routine server logs. Stripe and other third-party services may collect information needed to provide the services you choose.

Your choices and questions

You can change your analytics choice using the preferences button above and unsubscribe from emails at any time.

For questions about this notice, or to ask about information submitted through this site, contact Logan Doyle at Logan.missions@icloud.com. Information held by Stripe, Brevo, Google, Microsoft, or another provider may also be subject to that provider’s privacy policy and processes.
