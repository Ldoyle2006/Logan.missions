WHY I'M RAISING SUPPORT

Building a foundation for long-term missions.

When you partner with me financially, you're not just helping cover a trip or a single outreach. You're helping build a foundation for long-term missions.

Monthly support helps cover practical needs like rent, food, and transportation, while also allowing me to keep growing through discipleship, Diakonos classes, biblical education, and leadership training.

Part of my support will also be set aside for future schooling and ministry opportunities, so I can continue preparing for wherever God leads me next. My heart is to be faithful with what God has entrusted to me and transparent with the people who choose to partner with me.
