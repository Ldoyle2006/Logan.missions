October 30, 2026

Learning to See Like Jesus

Life in Kona, growing in faith, and learning to love people better.

Hi everyone,

I wanted to take a little time to share where I'm at and some of the heart behind what I'm doing. I'm in Kona, serving with YWAM and continuing to learn what it means to follow Jesus and love the people around me. Thank you for being part of that.

A Little of Life in Kona

I wanted this first letter to give you a glimpse of life here, from time around a table to the ocean and the coffee shop. I hope these photos help you feel a little more connected to life in Kona.

As I serve with different teams and take opportunities to learn, I want to keep growing in my relationship with Jesus. That's the heart behind this season, and behind the title of this letter: learning to see like Jesus.

Coffee and Connection

One part of life here that I've really enjoyed is working in the coffee shop. I get to serve people and grow in something I enjoy at the same time. It's an opportunity to keep learning about coffee while getting to connect with the people who come through.

The heart I want to bring to the coffee shop is pretty simple: make room for people. I want to be willing to listen, take time for a conversation, and share God's love in everyday moments. I want the way I serve people to reflect that.

Learning to See Like Jesus

I'm learning to ask what Jesus would do in the moments in front of me. I want to understand his heart for people and let that shape the way I see them and treat them.

Here in Kona, I've been going out to evangelize when I can, including taking time to talk with people who are homeless. In those conversations, I want to listen, love people, and see them the way Jesus does.

In worship, I've also been trying to really focus on God and keep my attention on him. Whether I'm worshiping, working, or talking with someone, I want to do my best to serve and love the people around me.

Something else I've been learning is how to work well across generations. Different worldviews can challenge me, and I want to learn how to love people and work alongside them without letting those differences offend me. I want to listen well and keep growing in how I relate to people of different ages and backgrounds.

Looking Ahead

Looking further ahead, I want to build a stronger foundation in the Bible so I can teach, disciple, and serve others. I'm preparing toward the Bible Core Course on Location, with September 2027 as the planned date, and I'd appreciate your prayers as I work toward that.

I'm also planning a trip to South America with my friend Jos. He's in the Netherlands and was a student on the DTS in New Zealand. We became really good friends, and I think of him as a brother.

We're planning to go independently, with the heart of hearing from God and serving people. I'd love your prayers for direction as we plan, and for us to stay open to where God leads and the people he puts in front of us.

Would You Pray With Me?

  • That I would keep growing in my relationship with Jesus, focus on him in worship, and learn to see people the way he does.
  • For opportunities to serve, listen, and share the love of Jesus with people here in Kona, including those who are homeless.
  • That I would learn to work well across generations, love people with different worldviews, and respond with understanding.
  • For wisdom, preparation, and provision as I work toward the Bible Core Course.
  • For Jos and me as we plan our trip to South America: that we would hear from God, follow his direction, and serve the people we meet.

Thank You for Being Part of This

Thank you for praying for me, supporting me, and taking the time to read this. I want these letters to help you feel connected to my life and the work you're helping make possible.

I'm grateful I get to share this journey with you. Thank you for being part of it.

Logan Doyle
