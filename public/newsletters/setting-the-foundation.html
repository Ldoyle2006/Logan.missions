Setting the Foundation

Week two in New Zealand: staff training, preparing for students, and grace over striving.

I’m officially in my second week here in New Zealand, stepping into staff training as we prepare for the upcoming Surf DTS at Northland/Zion Base (January–June).

These weeks have been full—not because everything is brand new, but because we are intentionally re-centering, reviewing, and re-establishing the values and foundations that shape how we live, lead, and disciple.

As We Prepare to Welcome Students

We’ve been spending time on team culture, communication, leadership, boundaries, and learning how to function well together so that we can steward this season and serve students in a healthy, intentional way.

Much of what we’re going over is a reminder of things many of us have learned in previous schools, but it’s been meaningful to revisit why we do what we do—not just how.

Our School Verse

Our school verse for this DTS is Psalm 105:1–4.

Give praise to the Lord, proclaim His name; make known among the nations what He has done... Look to the Lord and His strength; seek His face always.Psalm 105:1–4

This has already been shaping my prayers and posture as we prepare for students to arrive in just one week.

Potential Outreach Locations

As a school, we’re also praying into potential outreach locations including Indonesia, Fiji, Thailand, and India, while remaining open if the Lord clearly leads us elsewhere.

Alongside that, we’ve been talking through team dynamics—how to work well together, how to communicate clearly, and how to stay aligned in purpose as we move forward.

Grace Over Striving

This season has been inviting me into a deeper posture of humility, surrender, and discernment—learning to release control and trust God not just with outcomes, but with each step along the way.

I’ve been especially anchored in Ephesians 2:1–10, reminded that we are saved by grace, not by striving, and yet created for good works that God has already prepared. My prayer is simply to stay attentive, obedient, and faithful—doing my part while trusting God fully with the results.

Looking Ahead

Looking ahead, this season includes me staffing this DTS through June, and prayerfully preparing for what comes next, including my desire to do the BCC afterward. More than anything, my heart is to continue saying yes—to be obedient, teachable, and faithful in what God has placed in front of me.

Prayer Requests

  • For the students arriving next week—that their hearts would already be prepared for what the Lord has planned.
  • For wisdom, humility, and discernment as I help lead and walk alongside them.
  • That the Lord would continually position my heart rightly—grounded, surrendered, and healthy.
  • For unity, strength, and clarity within our staff team.

Thank You

Lastly, I want to say a sincere thank you to everyone who supports me through prayer, encouragement, and finances.

I wouldn’t be here without you. As this year continues, I am still looking for additional monthly supporters, as well as those who want to partner through prayer or words of encouragement. Every part matters, and I’m deeply grateful for the community that stands with me.

This is just the beginning of the year, and I’m expectant for what God is going to do in students, in teams, and in all the places He leads us.
