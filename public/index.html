A LIFE OF FAITH & SERVICE

WHY I’M HERE

Bearing Jesus’ image: loving each person, praying for every nation.

I believe lasting change grows through relationships. As I serve with YWAM, I have the privilege of learning from local leaders and ministry teams, building community, and sharing the hope of Jesus. Your prayers, encouragement, and generosity help make this shared work possible.

I’m continually inspired by the way Jesus loved each person with intention and compassion. He was also a man of persistent prayer, and I believe there is power when we pray. Carrying that kind of prayerful, intentional love is the image I strive to bear every day.

This calling has taken me from Montana and Hawaii to New Zealand, Indonesia, and Thailand. Along the way, I’ve served through youth and community programs, walked alongside young people, and helped create safe, Christ-centered spaces where people can gather, ask big questions, and grow together.

Every nation starts with an individual, and every life can be changed through the intentional love of Christ.
