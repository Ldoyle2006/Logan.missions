MY VISION

To see a generation know the love of the Father, encounter Jesus, and discover that they are known, loved, and created for a purpose.

I don't want to spend my life just talking about the love of Jesus. I want to live it. My heart is to reach children and young people, especially those who feel forgotten or have not had the opportunity to hear the gospel.

People are never just part of the story. They are the reason I go.

Hope for the Overlooked

Faithful Wherever God Leads

Preparing for long-term missions and remaining willing to serve wherever God calls.
